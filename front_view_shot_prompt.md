TASK
The attached image is the PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPH - the authority on the garment's design, fabric, construction and colour.
Produce ONE photorealistic e-commerce product photograph of a live human model wearing
that exact garment: the FRONT / HERO shot for a department store product page, in a clean
catalogue style. It must look like a real studio photograph - not like an edited product
photo and not like a render.

READ THE REFERENCE FIRST
Before generating, read the product photograph closely and note:
- what kind of product it is - a top, bottom wear (trousers, jeans, shorts, a skirt) or a
  full-length piece (a dress, a jumpsuit) - because that decides the framing;
- its exact colour - the precise hue, value and saturation, including any wash,
  fade or garment-dye variation;
- the pattern, if any - the motif, the repeat, and the true scale of that repeat
  relative to the garment;
- the fabric structure - the weave or knit, whether the surface is flat, ribbed,
  waffled, brushed, textured or slubby, and how matte or lustrous it is;
- the neckline or collar shape and how it is finished;
- the closure - its type, and the exact number and spacing of any buttons;
- the sleeve, the cuff and how each is constructed;
- the hem, the pockets, the seam lines, the topstitching and every trim;
- the cut and how heavy the cloth looks, that is, how it will hang on a body.

GARMENT FIDELITY - HIGHEST PRIORITY
Reproduce that garment EXACTLY. This outranks every other instruction in this brief.
- Match the colour precisely. Do not shift it warmer, cooler, lighter, darker or more
  saturated, and do not clean up a deliberate wash or fade.
- Keep the pattern identical in motif, repeat and scale. It must wrap the body and
  follow its contours, and stay aligned across the placket, yoke, shoulder and sleeve
  seams the way real cut-and-sewn fabric does.
- Keep the fabric's surface character visible. A textured cloth must stay textured -
  do not smooth a waffle, rib, slub or brushed surface into flat jersey.
- Keep construction identical: the same neckline or collar shape, the same closure with
  the same number of buttons in the same positions, the same sleeve and cuff, the same
  hem, the same pockets, the same seams, topstitching and trims.
- Do not redesign, restyle, modernise, slim down or otherwise improve the garment. Add
  nothing that is not in the reference - no prints, graphics, embroidery, contrast
  piping, extra pockets, drawcords or hardware.

SUBJECT
One adult woman - a professional fashion model, approximately 30 years old (late twenties
to early thirties): clearly a grown adult of about 30, not a teenager and not
middle-aged. A natural, healthy, approachable look and a natural build; the garment fits
true to size. Realistic skin that keeps its own texture and pores. Neat, natural hair.
Minimal, natural grooming and makeup.

POSE - FRONT / HERO
Standing upright and facing the camera squarely - straight on, or at most a slight
10-degree turn - weight evenly balanced, shoulders level and relaxed. Arms hang naturally
at the sides, held slightly away from the body so the garment's side seams and
silhouette read clearly; hands relaxed, not in pockets, not touching or covering the
garment. When the head is in frame: chin level, a pleasant, relaxed closed-mouth
expression, looking into the lens. For bottom wear the hands hang clear of the waistband,
fly and pockets, and the feet stand about hip-width apart so each leg reads on its own.
The entire front of the garment is unobstructed.

HOW THE GARMENT SITS
The garment is worn by a real human body and behaves like real cloth: shoulder seams sit
on the shoulder points, the sleeve breaks naturally at the wrist, soft folds gather at
the inner elbow and at the waist, and the hem hangs with the weight the fabric in the
reference actually has. Bottom wear sits at the rise it is cut for: the waistband level,
the fly and front lying flat, the legs or hem falling with a natural break. It fits the
way the reference garment is cut - not shrink-wrapped, not mannequin-stiff, not floating.
No pins, clips, tape or styling aids visible.

STYLING - supporting cast only, must not compete with the product
Keep the rest of the outfit plain and quiet so the product is the only thing that draws
the eye: simple, solid-colour, neutral supporting pieces that suit this product's
formality and season - for example plain denim or neutral chinos with a top, or a plain
neutral top with a bottom. When the product is bottom wear, the top is a plain, fitted,
solid neutral piece tucked in or ending above the waistband, so the waistband, belt loops
and closure stay fully visible front and back. Plain neutral footwear if the feet are in
frame. No bags, hats,
belts with logos or statement jewellery. Nothing patterned, branded or brightly coloured
anywhere else in the frame, and no supporting piece in the same colour as the product.

FRAMING
Square 1:1 frame, 1:1 aspect ratio (2048x2048 px). One person, centred horizontally. Decide what kind of
product the PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPH shows, then size the crop to it so the product is as large
as possible while completely visible:
- tops, shirts, knitwear, jackets and coats: from just above the top of the head to
  mid-thigh;
- dresses, jumpsuits and any full outfit: the full figure, from just above the top of the
  head to just below the feet;
- bottom wear - trousers, jeans, chinos, leggings, skirts and shorts: the LOWER BODY ONLY,
  from the belly down. The top edge of the frame crosses the midriff a little above the
  navel, so the whole waistband shows with only a narrow strip of the supporting top
  above it; the bottom edge sits just below the feet. The chest, shoulders, neck and head
  are outside the frame - this is a deliberate lower-body crop, not a full-figure shot.
  The legs fill the height of the frame, centred, with even background on either side.
  The back shot of bottom wear uses the same crop seen from behind, from the small of the
  back down.
Even margins left and right. The whole garment - neckline or waistband through to the
hem, and both cuffs - is inside the frame. Nothing important is cropped off.

LIGHTING AND SHADOWS
Flat, even, shadowless, high-key studio illumination - soft and uniform from the front,
as if the model stood inside a white light tent. Neutral daylight-balanced colour
(about 5500K). This is product photography, not a mood shot, so there are NO lighting
effects of any kind: no sunlight, window light or daylight streaks; no rim, hair, back,
edge or spot light; no coloured light; no glow, halo, bloom or light leak; no lens flare;
no vignette. The only tonal variation on the body and the garment is the gentle, natural
shading that lets folds and drape read as three-dimensional, and it never changes the
garment's true colour.
NO SHADOWS off the body: no cast shadow on the background, no floor or ground shadow, no
contact shadow under the feet, no drop shadow, no reflection, and no darkened edge or
outline around the silhouette.

BACKGROUND
Plain, in one of two colours decided by the product itself:
- pure white #FFFFFF (RGB 255, 255, 255) for any product that is not white;
- light grey #EBEBEB (RGB 235, 235, 235) if the product is white or
  near-white (white, off-white, ivory), so it never blends into the backdrop.
Perfectly flat and uniform from edge to edge - one single colour value everywhere behind
the model. No floor line, no horizon, no cyclorama curve, no wall corner, no gradient, no
texture, no vignette, no props and no set dressing. If the feet are in frame, they rest
on the same flat colour with no visible floor and no shadow.

CAMERA AND RENDER
Full-frame camera, 85mm lens at f/8, ISO 100, shot straight on at the garment's
mid-height - natural perspective with no wide-angle distortion. Everything in the frame -
the person and the whole garment - is tack sharp. Neutral white balance, true-to-life colour, natural
contrast. Retouched to commercial catalogue standard: clean but not plastic. Skin keeps
its real texture and pores. No HDR look, no heavy clarity, no oversharpening halos. High
resolution: crisp fabric texture, clean edges, and stitching and buttons clearly legible
at full size.

EXCLUSIONS
Do NOT include any of the following:
- Text of any kind: typography, captions, watermarks, brand names or logos, size or care
  labels, interior neck labels, hangtags, price tags, stickers, QR codes.
- Any trace of the reference images: no flat-lay or folded garment, no colour swatch or
  colour chip, no collage, no split frame, no side-by-side comparison, no inset, no
  thumbnail, no border or frame.
- Mannequins, dress forms, hangers, ghost-mannequin effects, headless bodies. (The
  lower-body crop for bottom wear is not a headless body - it is a crop that ends at the
  midriff. Never cut the frame at the neck or through the face.)
- Anatomy errors: extra or missing fingers or limbs, malformed hands, twisted joints,
  asymmetric eyes, duplicated people, a second person.
- Shadows of any kind on the background or floor, and any lighting effect: sun or
  sunbeams, window light, rim light, spotlight, coloured light, glow, lens flare,
  vignette.
- Any background other than the specified flat colour: no gradient, texture, floor,
  wall corner, horizon, props, furniture, plants, outdoor or domestic setting.
- Beauty-editorial excess: heavy makeup, glamour retouching, wet-look skin, dramatic
  hair styling.
- Motion blur, film grain, bokeh, colour fringing.
- Any non-photographic look: illustration, cartoon, painting, 3D render, CGI, AI-art
  gloss, plastic skin.
